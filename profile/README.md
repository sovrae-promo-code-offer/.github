## What each Sovrae fee means

Sovrae separates issuance from usage. Issuance is the one-time card price that promo code OFFER discounts. Usage fees apply after the card is live.

**Top-up fee** is the cut Sovrae takes when crypto is converted onto the card balance. A 1,000 USDT load costs 20 USDT on Virtual Privacy (2%) and 15 USDT on either physical card (1.5%). Timing: at load, before you spend.

**FX fee** is the 1.5% foreign-exchange charge on both virtual and physical products. Sovrae adds this fee when you pay a merchant in a currency that is not the card billing currency. A same-currency purchase does not trigger the FX line. Timing: at authorization on that purchase.

**Transaction fee** is the purchase fee. Sovrae Virtual Privacy bills $0.30 plus 1.5% of the ticket. Sovrae Physical Privacy and Physical Privacy Metal bill $0.10 plus 1.5% of the ticket. A $100 same-currency purchase costs $1.80 on virtual ($0.30 + $1.50) and $1.60 on physical ($0.10 + $1.50). Timing: at purchase, stacked with FX when the currencies differ.

**ATM withdrawal fee** is 2% and exists only on physical cards. A $200 cash withdrawal costs $4. Virtual Privacy has no ATM rail. Timing: at the ATM.

Sovrae fees stay the same after you use promo code OFFER. The code only changes the issuance price.
